UniFunds

A student-focused finance management application designed to help university students track expenses, manage budgets and improve their financial awareness.

Live Demo ↗ GitHub ↗
01

Overview

UniFunds is a student finance management web application. The main purpose of the application is to provide students with a simple way to record expenses, manage their budget and understand their spending habits.

02

Problem

University students often have to manage expenses such as food, transportation, education and other daily costs. Without proper tracking, it can become difficult to understand where their money is being spent.

03

Solution

UniFunds provides a centralized interface where students can record their expenses, organize financial information and monitor their spending.

04

Features

Expense Tracking

Record and manage daily expenses.

Budget Management

Monitor spending against a planned budget.

Financial Overview

View important financial information from a single dashboard.

Responsive Design

Use the application across desktop and mobile devices.
